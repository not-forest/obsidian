## Grammar (this, that)

| This (close to me)         | 이것  |
| -------------------------- | --- |
| That (close to you)        | 그것  |
| That (far from me and you) | 저것  |
이것은 제 가방이에요 - This is my bag
그것은 연필이에요 - That is a pencil
저것은 책상이에요 - That is a bench

| what | 무엇  |
| ---- | --- |
| what | 뭐   |
- 그것은 무엇이에요? - What is that thing?
- 이것은 책이에요. - This is a book.
- 저것은 뭐예요? - What is that thing?
- 저것은 가방이에요. - That is a backpack.

## Grammar (N1 이/가  N2 에  있다/없다)

Sentence construct when we are saying that some first noun **(N1) is in(not in) (N2)**. 
안나 씨**가** 교실**에 있어요** - Anna is in the classroom.
학생**이** 학교**에 있어요** - Student is in the school.
보이텍 씨**가** 교실**에 없어요** - Wojtek is not in the classroom.
학생**이** 학교**에 없어요** - Student is not in the school.
