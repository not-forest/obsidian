> **Secondary Synchronization Signal (SSS)** - second synchronization signal, which's timing can be known by **UE** only after detecting **PSS**. **SSS** is generated using **BPSK** modulated **gold sequence** of length 127, which is generating by multiplying two **BPSK** modulated **m-sequences** with cyclic shifts determined by **PCI**

After decoding both **PSS** and **SSS**, the **UE** can properly calculate the **PCI**.
